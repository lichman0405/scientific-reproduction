| DEV-DEFECT-001 | `src/scientific_reproduction/core/leases.py` (`_compare_and_unlink`), `core/locks.py` (`FileLock.release`, `_break_stale`) | **TOCTOU race at the expiry boundary (security review, HIGH/MEDIUM)**. The read-verify-then-unlink sequences are not atomic: a holder renewing/releasing at the exact expiry instant can have its fresh record removed by a claimer's takeover unlink that landed after verification (and vice versa). Worst case: transient dual ownership — two principals briefly believe they hold the lease/lock. Impact: never corrupts persisted state (the create step is always atomic create-if-absent, so at most one record exists on disk at any instant); the loser detects the loss on its very next lease/lock operation (record ≠ its grant → LeaseHeldError); requires a nanosecond interleaving between concurrent processes at the exact boundary. The deterministic tests (injected clock) cannot exercise the true wall-clock race. Proper fix: OS advisory locks (fcntl.flock / msvcrt.locking) on a never-unlinked per-object control file, or inode-bound removal (O_NOFOLLOW + fstat) — a cross-platform design change to a merged, reviewed component, outside every remaining v0.1 goal's frozen scope. | **ACCEPTED-RISK FOR v0.1, DOCUMENTED** — recorded in DEV-M1-G03 review as a known limitation; hazard window, invariant ("at most one record on disk; loser detects on next operation"), and detection semantics documented in module docstrings. Fix is a backlog candidate (v0.1.1; DEVELOPMENT_CHANGE_REQUEST-B if prioritized). No frozen acceptance criterion is modified. |
